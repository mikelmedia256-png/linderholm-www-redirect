What happens next

  1. Our volunteer coordinator calls you within 24 hours to find a shift that fits.
  2. You get a link to the private Team Linderholm Facebook group, where meetup spots and times are posted. Not on Facebook? We'll text or email you the details.

  4. On your first shift you go out with someone who has done it before.
