Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3

Alabama has no early voting. Polls are open 7 AM–7 PM. Bring a photo ID, and look for Linderholm under State Representative, District 20.

  • Request an absentee ballot by mail: Tue., Oct. 27
  • Request an absentee ballot in person: Thu., Oct. 29

Huntsville since 1991

A business leader for House District 20

James Linderholm moved to Huntsville in 1991 and built Huntsville Gymnastics into one of the region's top training gyms, with locations in Huntsville, Madison and Hampton Cove. Before that, he practiced law in London and Birmingham, Alabama.

District 20

Is Linderholm on your ballot?

District 20 covers southeast Huntsville and part of east Madison County: Monte Sano, the neighborhoods along Whitesburg Drive and Bailey Cove Road, Green Mountain, Hampton Cove and Owens Cross Roads, down to the Tennessee River.

If you live inside the red line, you can vote for Linderholm on Nov. 3. The state's voter information site confirms your registration and your polling place.
