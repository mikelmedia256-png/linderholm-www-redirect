Vote

Vote Tuesday, Nov. 3

Alabama has no early voting. Polls are open 7 AM–7 PM on Election Day.

What to do

  1. Register by Monday, Oct. 19

    It takes about five minutes on the Secretary of State's website.

    Register online (official state site, opens in a new tab)

  2. Find your polling place

    The state's voter information site confirms that you're registered and tells you where you vote.

    Check registration & polling place (official state site, opens in a new tab)

  3. Bring a photo ID

    Alabama requires a photo ID at the polls. The state lists which IDs count and how to get a free one.

    Photo ID rules (opens in a new tab)

  4. Look for Linderholm

    You'll find James Linderholm under State Representative, District 20. Look for the last name.

Voting absentee

If you can't get to the polls on Election Day, you may qualify to vote absentee. These are the deadlines.

Absentee voting rules (official state site, opens in a new tab)

District 20

Is Linderholm on your ballot?

District 20 covers southeast Huntsville and part of east Madison County: Monte Sano, the neighborhoods along Whitesburg Drive and Bailey Cove Road, Green Mountain, Hampton Cove and Owens Cross Roads, down to the Tennessee River.

If you live inside the red line, you can vote for Linderholm on Nov. 3. The state's voter information site confirms your registration and your polling place.
