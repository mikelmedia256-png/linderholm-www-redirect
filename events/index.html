Volunteer shifts and events

Every shift starts with a short training, and no one goes out alone.

Coming up

  • Oct31

    Final weekend canvass

    Sat, Oct 31 & Sun, Nov 1

    Shifts both days. It's the last weekend before Election Day, so we'll need the most help.

  • Nov3

    Election Day poll greeters

    Tue, Nov 3 · 7 AM–7 PM

    Stand outside an HD20 polling place and remind voters to find Linderholm on the ballot. Two-hour slots; we'll show you where to stand.

We post new dates every week. Sign up and we'll text you when the next shift is scheduled.

Every week

  • Doors

    Weekday door-knocking

    Monday–Friday · 10 AM–12 PM & 4–6 PM

    Two-hour shifts in pairs. There's a 30-minute training at the start, and on your first shift you go out with someone experienced.

  • Phones

    Phone bank (from home)

    Mon, Tue & Thu · 6–8 PM

    Call voters from home. We give you the script and the call list, and someone's on hand for questions.

  • Training

    New volunteer training (online)

    Wednesdays

    A 30-minute video call covering doors, phones and what to say. We'll send the link when you sign up.

For everyone's safety we don't post meetup addresses. Sign up and we'll text you where to meet.
